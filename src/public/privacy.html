1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Email address, password hash, and profile preferences (stored securely via Supabase/Firebase).
  • Usage Data: Feature interactions, session duration, and device type for performance optimization.
  • AI Processing Data: Text inputs submitted to AI summarization, quiz generation, and chat features. This data is processed in real-time and not permanently stored unless you explicitly save it.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve ZEQUI's core study features.
  • Sync your progress, notes, and preferences across devices.
  • Generate AI responses, summaries, and practice quizzes.
  • Send security alerts or service updates (opt-out available).

3. AI Processing & Explicit Consent

When you submit text for summarization, quizzes, or chat, you grant ZEQUI explicit permission to process that content through secure AI endpoints. This data is used solely to generate your requested output and is not used to train public AI models. You retain full ownership of your inputs and outputs.

4. Data Security & Storage

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. Authentication is handled by industry-standard providers. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for advertising.

5. Your Rights & Consent (GDPR / CCPA / APPI)

  • Access & Export: Request a full copy of your stored data.
  • Correction & Deletion: Modify or permanently delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw Consent: Opt out of non-essential data processing at any time.
  • Exercises of rights will be fulfilled within 30 days. Contact us at zequi.app.ai@gmail.com.

6. Cookies & Tracking Consent

ZEQUI uses essential cookies for authentication and session management. Non-essential analytics are disabled by default. You may manage cookie preferences in your browser settings. Continuing to browse constitutes consent for essential operations only. We do not sell, rent, or share behavioral data with advertisers.

7. Third-Party Services

ZEQUI integrates with secure third-party providers for authentication and AI processing. These partners are contractually bound to process data solely for ZEQUI's service delivery and comply with GDPR/CCPA standards.

8. Data Retention

Active account data is retained while your account exists. Deleted accounts and unsaved AI inputs are purged from our servers within 30 days. Saved notes and progress are retained until you request deletion.

9. Children's Privacy

ZEQUI is designed for students aged 13+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us immediately at zequi.app.ai@gmail.com for removal.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect legal or operational changes. Significant changes will be notified via in-app alert or email 14 days prior to enforcement.
